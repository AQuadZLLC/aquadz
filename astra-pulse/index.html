How a visit goes

Astra Pulse does the forms, the queue and the arithmetic. Your doctors decide.

  1. Name, age, blood group, insurance and a photo at the desk. The patient gets a digital health ID with a QR code, and a text message.

  2. The doctor sees past visits, allergies and vitals, and writes a digital prescription that goes straight to the pharmacy.

  3. Blood count, sugar, cholesterol and more, from the sample to the doctor's check, with the report sent to the patient by SMS and WhatsApp.

  4. Medicines, diet, exercise and tests in one plan, and a reminder the day before each follow-up visit.

  5. The bill builds itself from the visit. Insurance pays its share, and the patient pays the rest by cash, card or UPI.

Questions

Frequently asked

Something else? Email sales@aquadz.com.

Is it only for large hospitals?

No. It suits a nursing home as well as a multi-speciality hospital: you set up the departments you have, and add wards, maternity, the pharmacy or the ambulance as you need them.

Do patients have to install an app?

No. Tokens, lab reports and reminders arrive by SMS and WhatsApp, and the live queue opens on their phone.

Does it handle insurance?

Yes. The bill shows the insurer's share and the patient's share, and the claim is prepared from the same visit.

Which languages does it speak?

English, Tamil, Hindi, Malayalam, Telugu, Kannada and more, on the patient's screens and on your staff's.

Where is our data kept?

Hosted in your region, encrypted. You can export it at any time, and it is never used to train public AI models.

How do we start?

With a free demo on your own hospital's day. Then we set up your departments, doctors, services and prices, and train your team before you go live.
