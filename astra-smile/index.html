How a visit goes

Astra Smile does the forms, the reminders and the arithmetic. Your dentists decide.

  1. New patients fill in their details, medical history and ABHA number online, from your clinic website or a WhatsApp link. They get a patient ID and a check-in QR code.

  2. On an anatomical tooth chart the dentist marks fillings, root canals, implants, crowns and extractions, tooth by tooth, with the history of each.

  3. RVG X-rays, OPG and CBCT scans and intra-oral photos are saved with the patient, ready at the next visit and easy to show.

  4. Each step with its teeth and price, the total and the instalments. The patient signs the consent on screen and gets the prescription on WhatsApp.

  5. A GST invoice for every bill, instalments with their own reminders, and the receipt on the patient's WhatsApp.

Between visits, nothing slips

Lab work, aligners, reviews and check-ups follow up on their own, and every screen shows whose turn it is.

Also: your own clinic website with booking requests from the site and WhatsApp, and an emergency page for after hours.

Every chair and every branch, on one page

Questions

Frequently asked

Something else? Email sales@aquadz.com.

Do we need new computers?

No. Astra Smile runs in the browser on the computer at the front desk, a laptop, a tablet or a phone.

Do patients have to install an app?

No. Reminders, prescriptions and receipts arrive on WhatsApp. The patient app is there for those who want their visits, bills and X-rays on their phone.

Can we bring our old patient records?

Yes. Moving your old records in is part of the setup checklist, before you go live.

Which languages does it speak?

Tamil first, and English, Hindi, Malayalam, Telugu, Kannada and more, on the patient's screens and on your staff's, with large text for those who need it.

Where is our data kept?

Hosted in your region, encrypted. You can export it at any time, and it is never used to train public AI models.

How do we start?

With a free demo on your own clinic's day. Then we set up your chairs, dentists, treatments and prices, your team practises in the practice clinic, and you go live when the checklist is done.
